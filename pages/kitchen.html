The Kitchen

Our kitchen has everything you need for a great meal — from a quick breakfast to a full dinner.

Appliances

  • Gas range & oven — 5-burner stove with oven
  • Full-size refrigerator with freezer
  • Coffee station — Moccamaster drip coffee maker. Filters are provided, but please bring your own coffee beans or grounds.
  • Reverse osmosis drinking water — filtered tap by the sink
  • Toaster
  • Microwave

What's Stocked

We provide the basics to get you started:

  • Filters
  • Cooking oil and basic spices
  • Salt & pepper
  • Dish soap and sponge
  • Paper towels
  • Trash bags

Cookware & Utensils

You'll find a full set of:

  • Pots and pans
  • Baking sheets
  • Knife set
  • Cutting board
  • Cooking utensils (spatula, wooden spoons, tongs, etc.)
  • Mixing bowls
  • Can opener, bottle opener, corkscrew

Dining

The wood dining table seats 4 with comfortable chairs — perfect for meals or board games.

🛒 Nearest Grocery Stores
Close by: The Dollar General in Kachina Village has most everyday items, and the nearby gas station is the spot for treats and drinks.

For a full grocery run: Stock up in Flagstaff before heading to the cabin (~15 min north). Options include Fry's, Safeway, Sprouts, Natural Grocers, and Whole Foods.
