Booking Terms

  • Deposit: A 10% down payment holds your dates.
  • Cancel 5 or more days before check-in: Your deposit is fully refundable.
  • Cancel less than 5 days before check-in: The first night is non-refundable, and 50% of the remaining nights is refunded.
  • Leave early after check-in: You're responsible for the nights you've stayed, and 50% of any remaining nights is refunded.

These match the "Moderate" cancellation policy used on Airbnb. We'll confirm the total, the remaining balance, and payment details with you when you inquire.
