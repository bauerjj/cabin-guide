The Layout

Our cabin is a charming multi-level A-frame:

  • Main Level: Living room with gas fireplace and sofa bed, kitchen, dining area, bathroom
  • Loft (via spiral staircase): King bedroom, desk workspace, sitting area, balcony
  • Outside: Private hot tub, fenced yard, and fire pit seating

Sleeping Arrangements

The cabin sleeps up to 4 guests:

  • Loft bedroom: 1 king bed
  • Living room: 1 sofa bed that pulls out to a queen

Gas Fireplace

The living room has a high-output gas fireplace (Travis 4415 HO) — perfect for chilly Flagstaff evenings. It's controlled with the black remote. The remote shows the room temperature on its screen, and the fireplace beeps each time you press a button.

  • Power button: Turns the fireplace on and off
  • Thermometer button: Cycles through the heating settings:
    • Manual (shows "OFF") — you control the flame yourself
    • SMART — the fireplace automatically raises and lowers the flame to hold your target temperature. Use the up/down arrows to set it.
    • Standard thermostat (shows "ON") — the fireplace turns the flame fully on and off to hold your target temperature
  • Circle button (Mode): Cycles through flame height, blower, and accent light. Use the up/down arrows to change the one you've selected.
    • Flame height: 7 levels from low to high in manual mode. It can't be adjusted while in SMART mode.
    • Blower: Turn it on to push warm air out into the room faster. In manual mode it keeps running after the flame is off, so turn it off or press the power button when you're done.
    • Accent light: Turns the little night light inside the fireplace on or off
Fireplace safety
The glass and surrounding surfaces get very hot, so keep children and pets away. Keep blankets and other items well clear of it, and please turn it off when you leave the cabin or go to sleep.

Heating & Cooling

The cabin has a mini-split system (the wall unit in the living room) for both heating and cooling, controlled with the white BHI remote.

  • Remote is on the living room shelf
  • Aim the remote at the unit: You need to be fairly close to it and directly in front of it for the remote to work
  • Listen for the beep: The unit beeps when it receives a command. If you don't hear a beep, the new settings didn't take — move closer and try again.
  • Setpoint: The temperature you've set is shown on the unit itself
  • Modes (press MODE to cycle), left to right: Auto, Dehumidifier, AC (snowflake), Heat (sun)
  • Heat: Set to your comfort level (we recommend 68-72°F)
  • Cool: Flagstaff evenings are cool — open windows for natural AC in summer!
  • Please don't set below 65°F or above 75°F
Winter guests: The cabin warms up quickly, but the loft stays warmer than the main floor since heat rises.

Hot Tub

The hot tub is always on and ready — just remove the cover and enjoy!

  • Temperature is set to ~102°F
  • Always replace the cover when you're done (it keeps the heat in and debris out)
  • No glass near the tub — use plastic cups
  • Shower before entering
  • Steps are provided for easy entry
Pro tip: The hot tub under the stars on a clear Flagstaff night is absolutely magical. The dark skies here are internationally recognized!

Water & Bathroom

  • Drinking water: A reverse osmosis filtration unit is by the kitchen sink — fill your glass or bottle right from it
  • Heated floors in the bathroom keep your feet warm on cold mornings
  • Large walk-in shower with a tankless water heater, so hot water doesn't run out

Laundry

Full-size washer and dryer in the bathroom. Detergent pods are provided in the basket above the machines.

Spiral Staircase

The spiral staircase leads to the loft bedroom. It's sturdy but can be steep — take it easy, especially in socks!

Lighting

  • Loft: Switch at the top of the spiral staircase
